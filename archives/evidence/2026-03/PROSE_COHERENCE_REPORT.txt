ISSUE #9: Circular Logic in §7.2 Approach Statements
--------
Location: Lines 920-922, 931-933, 937-939
Pattern:
Each "Open Question" is followed by "Approach:" paragraph that proposes a 
solution within the current VCP framework.

Examples:
Q: "Can AI develop values outside human frameworks?"
A: "Version the UVC with explicit extension mechanisms."

Q: "How to handle genuine value conflicts?"
A: "CSM includes conflict detection and priority ordering."

Q: "Governance for the UVC ontology?"
A: "Establish multi-stakeholder governance committee."

Problem: These "Open Questions" are framed as unresolved issues, but the 
"Approach" sections describe solutions already in VCP (versioning, CSM, 
governance). This creates circular reasoning: if VCP already includes these 
mechanisms, why are these open questions? The section should either:
(a) Reframe as "Known Solutions to Previous Challenges"
(b) Ask genuinely unresolved questions without pre-existing answers
(c) Clarify what remains unresolved beyond these approaches

Status: STRUCTURAL CONFUSION - Section title (7.2 "Open Questions") implies 
genuine uncertainty, but content shows predetermined answers, suggesting content 
was condensed from longer discussion with removed doubt/debate sections.
