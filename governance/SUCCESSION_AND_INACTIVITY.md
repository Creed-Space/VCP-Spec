# Interim succession and inactivity procedure

The interim administrator may nominate a successor through a public signed
decision record. The successor affirms the interim limits, discloses conflicts,
and records repository and security-contact access without publishing secrets.

Inactivity means no repository, security-advisory, or public governance response
for 90 consecutive days after two documented contact attempts at least 14 days
apart. During inactivity, existing maintainers may merge reversible security,
availability, validation, archival, and dependency repairs after review. They
may not declare a permanent charter, change protocol maturity, resolve rights,
grant marks, create certification authority, publish a registry release, or
claim foundation transfer without separately established authority.

Re-establishment records the basis of authority, access handover, outstanding
appeals, embargoed matters, pending VEPs, and any emergency work performed.

Working signal: maintenance continues through absence, while high-authority
decisions remain visibly pending instead of being inferred from access alone.
