# Proposed patent, trademark, and certification policy

**Status:** discussion draft only. No legal effect.<br>
**Required gate:** authorized X016 legal and rights review, followed by recorded
governance adoption.

## Patent questions for review

1. Who may submit a disclosure and where is it recorded?
2. What knowledge standard and timing apply?
3. Does contribution imply any patent licence, and if so, on what terms?
4. Is defensive termination appropriate and legally supportable?
5. How are third-party claims, essential claims, and non-assert commitments
   handled?
6. What process manages confidential disclosures and conflicts?

No patent licence, non-assert promise, or defensive-termination term is granted
by this draft.

## Trademark questions for review

1. Who owns or is authorized to administer each name, logo, and mark?
2. Which descriptive references are permitted without approval?
3. Which logo, domain, event, merchandise, or certification uses require
   written approval?
4. What quality, compatibility, renewal, revocation, and appeals criteria apply?
5. How are nominative fair use and community criticism protected?
6. What happens if stewardship transfers to another entity?

No ownership claim, permission, certification mark, endorsement, or foundation
licence is created by this draft.

## Proposed process safeguards

Any adopted policy should publish the authorized owner, covered marks, permitted
uses, prohibited misleading uses, application route, decision deadline,
conflicts, fees if any, evidence standard, appeal route, version, and effective
date. Certification criteria should be versioned, technically reproducible,
time-limited, and separable from payment or membership.

Working signal: adopted policy language cites an authorized legal record and a
governance decision; before that, public pages make no certification or mark
permission claim.
