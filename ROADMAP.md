## Now: source and evidence integrity

1. Keep the VCP 3.1 source baseline, schemas, registries, SDK mirrors, and Demo
   copy synchronized by digest.
2. Close code-scanning alerts and repository-control gaps with hosted readback.
3. Maintain machine-readable document status, feature coverage, public claims,
   and residual risks.
4. Complete candidate-bound builds and validation without calling them an
   independent review or normative release.

## Next: authority and independent scrutiny

1. Ratify governance, publication authority, licensing, authorship, package
   names, trademark policy, contributor terms, and security decision roles.
2. Select one exact release candidate and freeze its source plus artifact
   digests.
3. Commission independent protocol, cryptographic, privacy, welfare, human
   accessibility, and cultural review.
4. Support a separately maintained implementation through the core profile and
   record disagreements as specification feedback.

## Then: immutable release and adoption

1. Resolve review findings and repeat candidate-bound validation.
2. Publish a signed immutable normative corpus, rendered accessible formats,
   conformance report, SBOMs, provenance, and errata route.
3. Publish packages only through protected trusted-publishing environments,
   then verify registry metadata and clean installation.
4. Hold an interoperability event and publish exact profile results.

## Conditional backlog

Internationalization, formal standards submission, certification, offline app
behavior, compliance profiles, broad observability, and funded adopter support
begin only when their named governance, evidence, privacy, or adoption trigger
exists.

## Explicit non-goals for this roadmap

No date is promised. No source-only candidate is promoted by this document. No
research, legal, rights, independent-review, deployment, or publication gate is
closed through planning prose.

## Working signal

The roadmap is working when each active item has a finite gate, completed work
moves out of “Now”, conditional work does not start without its trigger, and
public maturity claims never outrun authority or evidence.
